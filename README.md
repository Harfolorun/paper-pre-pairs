# Paper Pre-Pairs

*A pun on "prepares": it helps you prepare, one pair at a time.*

A "Match Two" game where **you** provide the pairs. Write your own word lists, or use folders of images or sounds, and practice them by matching. It's made for learning, especially languages, and it's **free to play**.

The game runs in your browser on phones and PCs. On Android it can also be installed as an app that works offline.

---

## Pairs

### Text pairs

Text pairs are added to the list on the **Pairs Bank** page. Every line is one **pair**, and its two parts are separated by a **comma**.

For example: `left, right` or `house, casa` or even `02-09-1945, End of WW2`.

- At least **5** pairs are needed to start a game.
- **[IM]** (import) loads pairs from a TXT file.
- **[EX]** (export) saves your pairs as a TXT file, with the date and the number of pairs in its name.

**Pairs Bank** is saved in your browser. **Export it with **[EX] regularly** as a backup, so you don't lose it when you clear your browser's *cookies and site data*.

### Image/Sound pairs

Select a **folder** of image or audio files. On some devices you have to select the files one by one. Each file is paired with the text in its **file name**.

- At least **5** files are needed.
- Images: **JPG** or **PNG**. Recommended size: 400×300 px.
- Audio: **WAV** or **MP3**.

## Game modes

| Mode | Pairs used | Goal | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Practice** | 20 pairs of your choice from the Pairs Bank | Match 60 pairs | None |
| **Challenge** | 20 pairs of your choice from the Pairs Bank | Match 60 pairs | **60 seconds**, and no hints! |
| **Repeat** | Every pair in the Pairs Bank | Match 120 pairs | None |
| **Match!** | All eligible files in the selected folder | Match 60 pairs | None |

In **Practice** and **Challenge**, you choose which rows of the Pairs Bank to play before the game starts, for example "1-20" or "21-40".

### Re-list Mistakes!

After a round of **Repeat**, press **Re-list Top 5 Mistakes**. The pairs from your top 5 mistakes of that round move to the **bottom** of your Pairs Bank, which puts them on the **last** row button in **Practice** and **Challenge**.

## Hints

Select the same card again after a wrong match: the game gives you a moment to think, then shows its correct match. Hints are off in **Challenge** mode.

## Where to get pairs

**Build your own list.** If you're just starting something new, like a language, start with a few pairs and add more as you learn. Just remember to export it regularly.

**Ask an AI chatbot.** Any AI chatbot can make a list for you in the right format. The trick is to describe the format exactly, because the game splits each line at the comma:

> Make a list of the 200 most common Spanish words with their English translations.
> Format: one pair per line, written as `Spanish word, English translation`.
> Use exactly one comma per line, only between the two parts. No numbering, no headings and no other text.
> Keep each part short (one to three words).

It doesn't have to be languages. For history, for example:

> Make a list of 100 notable events in world history with their dates.
> Format: one pair per line, written as `event, DD-MM-YYYY`.
> Use exactly one comma per line, only between the two parts. No numbering, no headings and no other text.
> Keep each event name short (up to five words).

Tips:

- Copy the answer into the Pairs Bank, or save it as a TXT file and import it with **[IM]**.
- **AI can make mistakes.** Check the list before you learn from it, especially dates, facts and translations.

## Options

- The **[?]** button on the main menu opens the full instructions inside the game.
- **Themes:** Light, Dark and True Black.
- **Text size:** Small or Medium, for both the menus and the cards. Cards also have an Extra Large option.

Your settings are saved in your browser on your device.

## Requirements

- A modern browser. **Chrome** is recommended, especially on Android, where some browsers can't open the folder or file pickers.
- Works on phones and PCs. The layout is made for phone screens; on most PC screens it fits into a square area in the middle of the window.
- No internet needed to play: all fonts, art and sounds are built into the game.

## Running it

- **Online:** at https://harfolorun.github.io/paper-pre-pairs
- **Android:** open the game in Chrome on your Android device and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** in the browser menu.
- **Locally:** download `index.html` and open it in your browser.

## Updates

To update the installed app on your Android device, open the game while connected to the internet, then close it. The new version loads the next time you open it.
